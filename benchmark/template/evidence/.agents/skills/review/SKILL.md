# Review

The compiler owns target resolution, host eligibility, overlap, and coverage. Do not repeat those structural checks.

For every `@evidence`, read the target, reason, and complete current host. Decide whether the host actually implements, represents, or proves the cited target and whether the reason states that exact relation. Reject any acknowledgement written, moved, consolidated, or invented only to pass the compiler.

For every `@evidenceExclude`, read the target, reason, and claimed owner or observable alternative. Verify that the target does not apply to this claim and that the reason states the concrete condition that would invalidate the exclusion. Reject any exclusion used only to discharge coverage.

Several hosts may truthfully cite one target. Do not consolidate them. A clean compiler gate proves structure, not the truth of a reason or host relation.

Finish the complete active-phase population even after finding a defect. Fix every cause, then repeat the full active-phase review from the beginning. Complete only after one full round finds no defect and the compiler gate is clean. Do not divide, cap, sample, or end review rounds by judgment.

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Read every active-phase acknowledgement, its complete host, and its target.
- [ ] Verified every `@evidence` relation is precise and truthful.
- [ ] Verified every `@evidenceExclude` against its target and claimed owner or observable alternative.
- [ ] Found no acknowledgement written, moved, consolidated, or invented only to pass the compiler.
- [ ] Repeated the complete active-phase review after the last fix and found no defect.
- [ ] Confirmed every remaining reason is precise and truthful and the current build is clean.

If any item is unchecked, repeat the full active-phase review after fixing its cause.
