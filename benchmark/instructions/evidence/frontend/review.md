# Evidence Frontend Review

Review the frontend Evidence annotations claim by claim.

1. Enumerate every active frontend claim and every annotation that belongs to it.
2. Inspect each annotation's host, target, and written reason.
3. Verify that the target exists and the reason truthfully explains the host's responsibility.
4. Verify that the annotations cover the claim without irrelevant, vague, false, or missing citations.

Fix every annotation defect and restart at item 1. Complete only when one full traversal needs no edit. Do not perform a general code review.
